manacles.rsi imports only brass_manacles from icons/obj/items_and_weapons.dmi.
The state was introduced in 32c68a60bf88b5e350f8ef78609ffccc45821483, committed
by Ashe Higgs; that identifies a source commit, not sole sprite authorship.
All 69 file-history revisions were checked for this state's pixels. Full
contributors, source and changes are retained in provenance.json. No separate
license exception was found in the selected file's metadata or introduction.
The inherited Bee security in-hand files contain no brass_manacles state.
Orbitra therefore retains the existing Objects/Misc/handcuffs.rsi hand/body
overlays with a runtime brass tint, under their original CC-BY-SA-3.0 notice.
